How it works

From scattered records to a review-ready pack

1Upload & profile

Policy, payslips, medical records, employer documents and correspondence.

2Build the timeline

Symptom onset, Date of Absence, first certificate, first notice and key insurer events.

3Read the policy

Identify the waiting period, disability test, notice requirements and other relevant wording.

4Map functional impact

Connect symptoms and limitations to the material duties of the role.

5Close evidence gaps

Identify missing records and who needs to provide them.

6Prepare the pack

Review everything together before anything is sent or escalated.

South African context

Built around the local claim journey

  • Date of Absence and waiting-period evidence
  • Employer-held records and job duties
  • Insurer claim-file and decision records
  • PAIA / POPIA record-access requests where relevant
  • Internal review and ombud escalation preparation

ClaimBuddy organises and drafts. It does not submit claims, replace legal or medical judgment, or guarantee an outcome.

Core principle

Function, not diagnosis.

Diagnosis explains what is happening medically. Income-protection claims usually turn on what you can or cannot reliably do in the material duties of your occupation.
