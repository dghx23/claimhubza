The market challenge

Income protection claims are rarely a single-document process.

A claim can sit across a policy, employer records, medical evidence, occupational information, correspondence and a decision process — with each participant holding a different part of the picture.

The result is often a fragmented experience: claimants are trying to understand what is needed, professionals are working from different records, and important evidence or context can arrive late or in the wrong format.

01Information is scattered

Policy wording, medical evidence, employment records and correspondence often live in different places.

02Roles are disconnected

Claimants, employers, advisers, clinicians and claims teams may each see only part of the case.

03Evidence can be hard to translate

Clinical facts do not automatically explain functional impact, material duties or the policy test.

04Processes are difficult to follow

Requests, deadlines, missing documents and decisions can become hard to track across email and separate systems.

01 · The context

Income protection claims involve more than one person — and more than one type of evidence.

Policies can be arranged through an employer or held individually. Either way, the claim can involve policy wording, employment records, medical evidence, functional impact, timelines and insurer correspondence.

Individual cover

Held for the individual. The claimant or adviser may hold the policy and starting documents.

  • Policy wording and schedule
